Correlation matrix · design mock-up (candidate 6)

Synthetic data, 220 records, five measures with known relationships: strong (Income–Spend), moderate (Spend–Visits), curved negative (Visits–Days since visit), none (Basket size), and one outlier (top-right of Income–Spend). Below the diagonal: every pair as a scatter with a thin least-squares line. On the diagonal: each measure's histogram, labelled with its name (it labels the row and the column, so no headers). Above the diagonal: the coefficient, sized and tinted by strength, blue positive, orange-red negative, grey near zero. The orange dot is one record, highlighted in every scatter (what hover or selection would do).

  • Ink: no cell borders or gridlines; tick labels only on the outer edges; points small and translucent.
  • Pearson vs Spearman: compare Visits–Days since visit (curved) and Income–Spend (outlier).
