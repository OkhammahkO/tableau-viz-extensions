How they work

  • Install: download a .trex file, then in Tableau choose Show Me → Viz Extensions → Access Local Viz Extensions. Tableau 2024.2 or later.
  • Private: your data stays in Tableau. The extensions draw inside the worksheet and send nothing anywhere.
  • Tableau Cloud or Server: an admin first adds https://okhammahko.github.io to the site's extension allow list.
  • Open source: MIT licence; ideas and bug reports welcome in the issue tracker.
