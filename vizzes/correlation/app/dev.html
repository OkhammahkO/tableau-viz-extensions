Correlation matrix live demo

Drag the frame's corner to resize. Hover a point to find the record in every scatter; click to select (Ctrl+click adds); drag a rectangle in a scatter to select what's inside; hover or click a histogram bar for its value range; hover a legend member to bring its colour forward; click a density cell to select its records. Hover a number for r, ρ, n, the formula and r per colour; click a number or the ⤢ on a hovered scatter (or double-click it) to look closer (it brushes too: drag, hover or click its histogram bars), Esc to go back; pause over a scatter for a preview in the top-right corner (click it to open).
